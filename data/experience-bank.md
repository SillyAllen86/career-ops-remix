## Best use in Career-Ops
Use a 3-layer structure:
1. **Master CV** - comprehensive but still public-facing and credible.
2. **Experience Bank** - proof points, nuances, sensitive context, and interview ammunition.
3. **Role Overlay** - 6 to 12 bullets selected from the bank for a specific job.

## Tailoring rules
- If the role is strategy / architecture heavy, pull SAP dependency mapping, operating model design, integration strategy, and executive artefacts.
- If the role is product / platform heavy, pull Elsevier product governance, roadmap prioritization, analytics adoption, and regulated SaaS depth.
- If the role is AI / data heavy, pull retrieval, knowledge workflow, AI governance, and document-grounding examples.
- If the role is operations / finance heavy, pull forecast-to-actual, budgeting, pricing model, and decision-support points.
- If the role is clinical operations heavy, pull outsourced trial operations leadership, site feasibility analytics, and protocol/reporting automation.
- If the role explicitly names clinical systems, pull the CTMS/eTMF/RIMS evidence and describe it precisely as user-side, BA-side, and prioritization-stakeholder experience unless direct platform ownership can be defended.
