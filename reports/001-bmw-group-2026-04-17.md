## C) Level and Strategy

### Level read

- **JD level:** senior architect with technical leadership
- **Your natural level for this archetype:** senior/lead in architecture, transformation, and cross-functional systems design
- **Risk:** BMW may read the profile as transformation-heavy rather than platform-heavy unless the application materials sharpen the AI/data architecture signal immediately

### Sell senior without lying

Use this positioning:

> I have spent 13+ years designing and improving complex, compliance-sensitive operating environments where architecture decisions have to survive real-world constraints, cross-functional dependencies, and auditability. Over the last two years, I have also built AI-enabled workflow, retrieval, knowledge graph, and diagramming tools myself, which lets me bridge strategic architecture with practical implementation.

Emphasize these points:

- You already operate at enterprise complexity.
- You already design interfaces, orchestration, and target-state systems.
- You already work where compliance, governance, and sensitive data matter.
- You are not trying to become an architect; you already are one.
- The new element is domain transfer into enterprise AI/data platforms, not a reinvention of your career.

### If they downlevel

- Accept only if the scope still includes architecture and cross-unit influence.
- Ask for promotion criteria up front.
- Ask for a 6-month review tied to platform ownership and delivery outcomes.
