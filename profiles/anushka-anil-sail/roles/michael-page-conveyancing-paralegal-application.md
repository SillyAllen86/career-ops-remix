# Michael Page Conveyancing Paralegal -- Application Notes

## Why This Role Is More Difficult

This role asks for practical conveyancing experience, which is not clearly evidenced on Anushka's CV. She can offer strong general paralegal support skills, but she should not be presented as someone who already has hands-on end-to-end conveyancing experience.

## Points To Emphasize

- Legal drafting and document review.
- Strong organisation, file handling, and deadline management.
- Experience liaising with clients and supporting legal teams.
- Capability in legal research, documentation control, and matter support.

## Points To Handle Carefully

- Do not claim conveyancing transactions, searches, title work, exchange, completion, remortgages, or Land Registry experience.
- This application should rely on transferability, not equivalence.
